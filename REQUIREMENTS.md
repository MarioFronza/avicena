# Avicena Requirements

Original project requirements for Avicena, a clinic management system built for the
Programação II course at UDESC. Translated and reorganized from the original
`AvicenaRequisitos.txt` (Portuguese, ISO-8859-1) into English Markdown, grouped by
entity and process rather than a flat numbered list. No requirement or business rule
was added, dropped, or changed in meaning during this rewrite.

## Functional Requirements

The system must:

- Maintain patient records.
- Maintain doctor records.
- Maintain attendant (receptionist) records.
- Maintain nurse records.
- Schedule consultations (appointments).
- Generate a patient's consultation history.
- Generate a list of daily consultations.
- Maintain diagnoses.

## Entities and Their Data

### Patient

Name, CPF, code, phone, address, marital status.

Patients are registered by an attendant.

### Doctor

Name, CPF, code, phone, address, marital status, CRM (medical license number),
specialization.

### Attendant

Name, CPF, code, phone, address, marital status, work hours, salary, overtime hours,
labor card number.

### Nurse

Name, CPF, code, phone, address, marital status, education/qualification, hours
completed (training).

## Process Rules

- A consultation is registered by an attendant.
- Both the doctor and the nurse involved in a consultation can produce diagnoses.
- Only a doctor can generate a prescription.
- A prescription is generated by the doctor after a diagnosis.
- An attendant generates a patient's consultation history.
